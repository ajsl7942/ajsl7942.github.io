6. 정보를 지우는 방법

  • 광장에서만 내리기: 광장 탭의 "광장에 내 캐릭터 올리기"를 끄면 됩니다.
  • 휴대폰의 정보 지우기: 앱을 삭제하면 휴대폰에 저장된 정보가 지워집니다. 앱을 삭제해도 서버의 정보는 남으므로, 서버 정보까지 지우려면 삭제하기 전에 "친구 코드 없애기"를 먼저 눌러 주세요.
  • 앱 없이 요청하기: 앱을 이미 지웠다면 위 문의 주소로 친구 코드를 알려 주세요. 확인 후 서버에서 지웁니다. 구글 계정으로 맡긴 데이터는 앱을 다시 깔아 로그인한 뒤 지우거나, 문의 주소로 요청해 주세요.

Privacy Policy for 귀여운것 키우기 (Cute Something)

Effective date: November 17, 2026

"귀여운것 키우기" (the "app") lets you turn your own photo into a character and take care of it.
This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

  • Operator: Kim Sanghun (김상훈)
  • Contact: ajsl7942@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • There is no sign-up. We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.
  • The app shows rewarded ads (Google AdMob) only when you choose to watch one. To serve ads, Google collects your advertising ID and device information (see section 5). We do not sell your information.
  • To learn which features are used most, the app uses an analytics tool (Google Analytics for Firebase). It records usage events such as "fed the pet" or "sent on a hunt" only; your character's name, photos and message text are never sent (see section 5).
  • Nothing is sent to our server until you create a friend code or sign in with Google. Your character photos and game progress stay on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Google (optional), your game progress, photos and sounds are backed up to our server so you can continue after reinstalling or switching phones. Only you can restore your backup.
  • When you create a friend code, your character's name and photos are uploaded and can be seen by anyone who knows that code.
  • If you turn on "Show my character in the plaza", your character becomes visible to everyone who uses the app. This is off by default.
  • Tapping "Delete friend code" removes your information from our server immediately.

2. Information stored only on your phone

4. Who can see your information

  • People who know your friend code: they can see your character's name, photos and accessory, and add you as a friend. When someone adds you, their character also appears in your friend list.
  • The Plaza (only if you turn it on): your character's name, level, status and small photo are visible to everyone who uses the app. Your friend code is not shown in the Plaza.
  • People you message: they see your message along with your character's name and small photo.
  • The operator: when a report is filed, we review the reported character's photo and the reported message.

When a friend downloads your character, the photos are saved on that friend's phone. We cannot delete copies already saved on someone else's phone, even after you delete your friend code or change your photos.

5. Sharing with third parties

  • We do not sell your information. Another company processes information only in the case below.
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC): when you sign in with Google, Google verifies your account. The app receives only a value identifying the account and does not send your game data to Google.
  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase, Google LLC): to improve the app, we collect which features you use (for example feeding, washing, sleeping, sending on jobs, opening the customize or friends screens, watching ads), your character's level, and how long and how often the app is used. Google's analytics SDK also collects an identifier assigned to each app install, device type, OS, language and approximate region (country). Your character's name, photos, sounds, message text and friend code are not sent. Google collects this directly, not through our server, and handles it under its policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Collection stops when you delete the app.
  • Ads (Google AdMob, Google LLC): to show rewarded ads, Google's ads SDK collects your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Google collects this directly, not through our server, and handles it under its policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can turn off personalised ads by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • We may disclose information only when required by law through proper legal process.
  • The server is operated directly by us and is located in South Korea. Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS) and is routed through the network of Tailscale Inc., where it travels only in encrypted form.

6. How to delete your information

  • Delete everything on our server: in the app, open Friends > Friends tab > "Delete friend code". Your character, photos, friend links, requests, messages, blocks and reports are deleted from the server immediately.
  • Delete your backup and account: open "Protect my data" at the top of the Customize screen and tap "Delete account and backup". The backed-up progress and files and the value identifying your account are deleted from the server immediately. Data on your phone stays. "Sign out" alone keeps the backup.
  • Leave only the Plaza: turn off "Show my character in the plaza" in the Plaza tab.
  • Delete what is on your phone: delete the app. This does not delete server data, so tap "Delete friend code" first if you want that removed too.
  • Without the app: if you have already deleted the app, email the contact address above with your friend code and we will delete your data after confirming. For a Google backup, reinstall and sign in to delete it, or email us.
  • Ad information: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
